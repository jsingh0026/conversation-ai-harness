# Marisol Bay Neighborhoods

Marisol Bay is a city of distinct communities, each with its own character. Lumina Realty serves all five of these neighborhoods and knows them well. Here is a short profile of each.

## The Cove

The Cove is Marisol Bay's premium waterfront neighborhood. Homes here enjoy water views and direct access to the shoreline, making it one of the most sought-after areas in the city. If you dream of morning coffee by the water, this is your place.

## Old Town

Old Town is the historic, walkable heart of Marisol Bay. Tree-lined streets, character homes, and independent shops and cafes give it a timeless charm. It suits those who love history and the convenience of getting around on foot.

## Maple Heights

Maple Heights is a family-friendly suburban neighborhood. With quieter streets, more space, and a welcoming community feel, it is a favorite for families looking to settle in for the long term.

## Dockside

Dockside is home to modern condos and a lively atmosphere that appeals to young professionals. Contemporary buildings, easy amenities, and a fresh, energetic vibe define this part of the city.

## Vista Ridge

Vista Ridge is the luxury hillside neighborhood, offering elevated homes with sweeping views over Marisol Bay. It is the choice for buyers seeking privacy, space, and premium finishes.
