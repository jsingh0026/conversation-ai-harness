# Privacy and Your Data

Your trust matters to us. This page explains what information Demo Realty collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

## What We Store

When you work with us, we store your contact details, such as your name, phone number, and email address, along with basic information about what you are looking for. This is kept in our customer relationship management (CRM) system.

## Why We Store It

We keep your information for one reason: to serve you well. Having your details on hand lets your agent share suitable listings, arrange viewings, follow up on offers, and stay in touch throughout your buying, selling, or rental journey.

## We Do Not Sell Your Data

Demo Realty does not sell customer data. Your information is used to serve you, not to be traded or sold to third parties.

## Updating or Removing Your Information

You are in control of your information. If you would like to review, update, or remove your details from our CRM, simply reach out:

- Email: hello@demorealty.example

We will promptly help you correct or delete your information on request.

## Sharing With Partners

At times, serving you may involve trusted partners, for example connecting you with a lender such as Harbor Trust Bank or Marisol Credit Union for pre-approval. We only share what is necessary to help you, and only in the course of assisting with your request.

## Questions

If you have any questions about how we handle your data, we are happy to talk it through. Reach out any time during our office hours, Monday to Friday 9am to 6pm and Saturday 10am to 4pm.
