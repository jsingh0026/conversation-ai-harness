# Our Services

Demo Realty offers a full range of residential real-estate services across Marisol Bay and its surrounding county. Everything we do centers on homes and the people who live in them.

## Buying

We represent buyers at no charge. Buyer representation is free, because the seller's side covers agent compensation. Your dedicated agent helps you define your search, tour homes, evaluate value, negotiate offers, and manage the path to closing. We recommend mortgage pre-approval before touring so you can move quickly and confidently.

## Selling

We guide sellers from first conversation to closing day. This includes a comparative market analysis to price your home, staging advice, professional marketing, showing coordination, offer negotiation, and closing support. Our seller commission is 5% of the sale price.

## Rentals

We help renters find homes at no cost to the renter, and we manage rental properties for landlords. Our property management fee is 8% of the monthly rent, covering tenant placement, coordination, and ongoing support.

## Relocation Help

Moving to Marisol Bay from elsewhere? Our agents offer relocation guidance, including neighborhood orientation across The Cove, Old Town, Maple Heights, Dockside, and Vista Ridge, so you can choose the community that fits your life.

## First-Time Buyers

Through our "Demo First Key" program, first-time buyers receive dedicated guidance plus a closing-cost credit of up to $2,500 for those who qualify.

## What We Do Not Offer

Demo Realty is residential only. We do not handle commercial real estate, and we are not a lender, so we do not provide mortgages or loans directly. We also serve only Marisol Bay county. For financing, we partner with Harbor Trust Bank and Marisol Credit Union.
