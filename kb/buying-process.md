# The Buying Process

Buying a home with Demo Realty is designed to be clear and unhurried. Here is how it typically unfolds. Remember, buyer representation is free to you.

## Step 1: Get Pre-Approved

Before touring homes, we recommend getting a mortgage pre-approval. Pre-approval tells you what you can comfortably afford and shows sellers you are a serious buyer. Demo Realty is not a lender, but we partner with Harbor Trust Bank and Marisol Credit Union and can point you to them.

## Step 2: Meet Your Buyer's Agent

Your dedicated buyer's agent learns your needs, budget, and preferred neighborhoods, whether that is waterfront living in The Cove or a family home in Maple Heights. Your agent works on your behalf throughout the process.

## Step 3: Search and Tour

We curate listings that match your criteria and arrange viewings. A standard viewing is a 30-minute slot booked through your agent. Please bring a photo ID. Same-day viewings are possible subject to availability.

## Step 4: Make an Offer

When you find the right home, your agent helps you craft a competitive offer, advises on price and terms, and negotiates with the seller's side on your behalf.

## Step 5: Under Contract

Once an offer is accepted, we help coordinate inspections, appraisal, and your lender's underwriting. Your agent keeps every party moving toward the finish line.

## Step 6: Closing

At closing, you sign the final paperwork and receive your keys. First-time buyers may qualify for a closing-cost credit of up to $2,500 through our "Demo First Key" program.

## A Note on Scope

We serve residential buyers in Marisol Bay county only. We do not handle commercial real estate or provide loans directly.
