# Financing and Mortgages

Understanding your financing is one of the most important steps in buying a home. Here is how Lumina Realty helps, and where our role ends.

## Lumina Is Not a Lender

To be clear: Lumina Realty is a real-estate brokerage, not a lender. We do not provide mortgages or loans directly, and we do not set interest rates. What we do is help you understand the process and connect you with trusted local lenders.

## Our Lending Partners

We partner with two respected local institutions:

- Harbor Trust Bank
- Marisol Credit Union

Both know the Marisol Bay market well. You are always free to choose your own lender; these are simply partners we trust.

## Why Pre-Approval Matters

We strongly recommend getting a mortgage pre-approval before you start touring homes. Pre-approval helps in three ways:

- It tells you what price range you can comfortably afford.
- It makes your offers stronger, since sellers see you as a serious, ready buyer.
- It speeds up the path to closing once you find the right home.

## General Financing Steps

1. Gather your financial documents, such as income, assets, and debts.
2. Apply for pre-approval with a lender like Harbor Trust Bank or Marisol Credit Union.
3. Receive your pre-approval letter and share it with your Lumina agent.
4. Shop for homes within your approved range.
5. Finalize your loan (underwriting) once your offer is accepted.

## First-Time Buyers

First-time buyers may qualify for a closing-cost credit of up to $2,500 through our "Lumina First Key" program, which pairs nicely with a lender pre-approval.
