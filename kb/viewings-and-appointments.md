# Viewings and Appointments

Seeing a home in person is where the search comes to life. Here is how viewings work at Demo Realty.

## How to Book

Viewings are booked through a Demo Realty agent. You can start by calling us at (555) 012-8899 or emailing hello@demorealty.example, and we will connect you with an agent who can schedule your tours.

## What to Expect

A standard viewing is a 30-minute slot. That is enough time to walk the home, ask questions, and get a feel for the space. If you want to see several homes in a day, your agent can arrange back-to-back appointments.

## Bring a Photo ID

Please bring a valid photo ID to your viewing. This is a standard courtesy that helps keep everyone, including current occupants and sellers, safe and comfortable.

## Same-Day Viewings

Same-day viewings are sometimes possible, subject to availability. If you spot a home you love and want to see it quickly, let your agent know and we will do our best to make it happen.

## Office Hours

Our office is open Monday through Friday 9am to 6pm, and Saturday 10am to 4pm. We are closed on Sunday. Viewings are arranged around these hours and your agent's schedule.

## Cancellations and Rescheduling

Plans change, and that is okay. If you need to cancel or reschedule a viewing, please let your agent know as early as you can. Advance notice lets us respect the seller's time and free up the slot for others.

## Scope

Viewings are for residential homes in Marisol Bay county. We do not arrange commercial property tours.
