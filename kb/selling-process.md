# The Selling Process

Selling your home with Demo Realty is a partnership. We handle the details so you can focus on your next move. Our seller commission is 5% of the final sale price.

## Step 1: Listing Consultation

We start with a conversation about your goals and timeline, then walk through your home to understand what makes it special. This is also when we explain our services and commission structure.

## Step 2: Pricing and CMA

We prepare a comparative market analysis (CMA) that looks at comparable homes in your neighborhood to recommend a competitive, realistic asking price. Accurate pricing attracts the right buyers.

## Step 3: Staging and Preparation

We offer staging advice to help your home show at its best, from decluttering to small touch-ups that make a strong first impression during viewings.

## Step 4: Marketing

We create professional marketing for your listing, including photography and exposure to buyers searching in Marisol Bay county. Our local focus means we reach the audience most likely to fall in love with your home.

## Step 5: Showings and Offers

We coordinate viewings, typically 30-minute slots, and field inquiries from interested buyers and their agents. When offers arrive, we review each one with you and negotiate on your behalf.

## Step 6: Closing

Once you accept an offer, we manage inspections, appraisal coordination, and paperwork through to closing day. At closing, ownership transfers to the buyer and you receive your proceeds.

## Commission

Our total seller commission is 5% of the sale price: 2.5% to the listing side and 2.5% to the buyer's agent side. See our fees page for full details. We handle residential properties only, not commercial real estate.
